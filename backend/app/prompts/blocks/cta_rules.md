CTA rules: the closing scene is a binary question — one question offering two opposite judgements of the same policy, joined by "atau", where each side is short (at most 5 words) and a viewer can answer in at most 5 words. Pattern example (a pattern to follow, not a topic to copy): "hemat subsidi atau cuma ganti kemasan doang?" Never use a generic closing such as "menurut lo gimana?" as the only closing — a generic question earns no comments because it hands the viewer nothing to pick. The exact same binary question must also be the comment question in the video description, word for word; do not invent a different question for the description.
