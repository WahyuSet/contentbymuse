Pacing: narration is spoken at roughly 2.3 words per second (kata per detik). The whole script must land between {{ target_min_s }} and {{ target_max_s }} seconds, which means roughly 70 to 90 spoken words in total. Use 8 to 14 scenes. The first scene (the hook) must finish within 3.5 seconds, so keep it to at most 7 words. Every scene carries 3 to 16 spoken words; a scene longer than 12 words feels dragged, so split it instead. Leave no dead air: each scene must add new information, a turn, or a payoff. For political topics, 2 of the scenes are impact beats (about 8 spoken words each) carrying the sebab-akibat chain to the viewer; their words come out of the main scenes' budget, so the total word budget above stays unchanged.
