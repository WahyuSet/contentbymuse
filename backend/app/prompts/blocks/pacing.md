Pacing: narration is spoken at roughly 2.3 words per second (kata per detik). The ONLY duration limit is an upper bound: the total estimated duration must stay below {{ target_max_s }} seconds — with the default limit of 45 seconds that means roughly a maximum of 100 spoken words in total. There is no minimum duration and no minimum word count: a 20-second video is completely valid. Use 8 to 14 scenes. The first scene (the hook) must finish within 3.5 seconds, so keep it to at most about 7 words. Aim for 8 to 14 spoken words per scene, each written as a complete sentence (see the spoken-Indonesian block for the complete-sentence rule); a scene longer than 12 words still feels heavy, so split it instead of cramming — and never compress a scene into headline fragments just to save words. Leave no dead air: each scene must add new information, a turn, or a payoff. For political topics, 2 of the scenes are impact beats carrying the sebab-akibat chain to the viewer; their words come out of the same total budget, so the maximum above stays unchanged.
