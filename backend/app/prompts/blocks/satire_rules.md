Satire safety (wajib): the satire targets behavior and offices, never real people. Never use the real name of any politician, official, party figure, party, institution spokesperson, or company in the narration or visuals — refer to roles instead ("anggota dewan itu", "menteri tersebut", "oknum pejabat", "partai penguasa" tanpa nama). Every factual claim about a real event must be a verifiable, widely reported fact; when a detail is disputed or still developing, say so in the narration ("katanya", "menurut laporan") or leave it out. No hate speech toward any ethnic, religious, or social group; the joke is always the corrupt or absurd behavior, never an identity. Keep the tone playful, not enraged.
