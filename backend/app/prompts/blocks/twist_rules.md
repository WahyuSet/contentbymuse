Twist rules: for political and news topics the hook first delivers the popular claim — the headline version or the official claim exactly as the public hears it ("popular claim") — and the script then corrects it in exactly one scene with beat "twist", placed after the main explanation scenes and before the first impact scene. The twist must correct that same popular claim explicitly, in spoken words, using a correction marker such as ternyata, padahal, sebetulnya, faktanya, salah paham, bukan buat, justru, or bukan begitu — never let the correction be implied. The twist scene (or the scene right around it) must also carry one fair steelman sentence that honestly states the policy's good side before the criticism lands. Pattern example (a pattern to follow, not a topic to copy): the popular claim says LPG is "30% lebih murah", and the twist corrects it — "ternyata yang turun beban subsidi negara, harga rumah tangga tetap". The correction is a factual claim, so put it into `claims_to_verify` like every other fact; if the correction cannot be verified, drop it — never invent a correction to complete the twist.
