Sound effects: each scene may carry up to 2 `sfx_cues`. A cue is {"sfx": "<id>", "word": "<exact word from this scene's voiceover>", "gain": optional 0.05-1.0}. The word must appear verbatim in the voiceover; the effect plays when that word is spoken. Catalog: ding (bright chime, a reveal or a pointed question), boom (low thud, big or bad news lands), whoosh (soft sweep), pop (tiny surprise), cash (coin chime, money is mentioned), fall (pitch slide down, prices fall or plans collapse), drum (short sting, punchline), scratch (record scratch, an absurd twist), sparkle (rising shimmer, good news or hope). Use effects sparingly — most scenes need none; save them for the hook, the turn, and the payoff.
