Rules for every idea:
- The hook greets or provokes the viewer directly and never opens with the answer or its explanation.
- The angle states the concrete answer the video will give, in plain words; never name the curiosity mechanism itself (write the fact, not "hidden logic" or "curiosity gap").
- Titles are 20 to 90 characters and no two titles may share most of their words.
- Prefer everyday, visual, slightly surprising subjects over abstract or textbook topics.
- Never promise medical, financial, or legal advice; health topics stay general knowledge.
